# L8. Algorithm pseudocode

Write a complete document with the preconditioned conjugate gradient method as a numbered algorithm, using the `algorithm` and `algpseudocode` packages.

- A floating `algorithm` with the caption "Preconditioned conjugate gradients" and the label `alg:pcg`; numbered lines (`\begin{algorithmic}[1]`).
- Inputs (`\Require`): matrix $A$, right-hand side $b$, preconditioner $M$, initial guess $x_0$, tolerance $\varepsilon$. Output (`\Ensure`): approximate solution $x$.
- The body is the textbook method: the initial residual $r_0 = b - A x_0$, preconditioned residual $z_0 = M^{-1} r_0$ and direction $p_0 = z_0$; a loop with `\While` that stops once the residual norm falls below the tolerance; inside, the step length $\alpha_k$, the updates of $x$ and $r$, the new $z$, then $\beta_k$ and the new direction. Finish with `\Return`.
- At least one `\Comment`.
- A sentence after the algorithm that refers to it: "Algorithm 1 needs one preconditioner solve per iteration", with the number through `\ref`.
