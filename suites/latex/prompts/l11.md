# L11. TikZ geometry: exact coordinates

Write a complete document with one `tikzpicture` of the trigonometric functions on a circle of radius 2 cm, for the angle theta = 30 degrees:

- A circle of radius 2 cm centred on the origin, with x and y axes (arrow tips) through it.
- Named coordinates (use `\coordinate`, with exactly these names):
  - `O`: the centre.
  - `A`: where the circle meets the positive x axis.
  - `P`: the point on the circle at angle theta.
  - `Q`: the foot of the perpendicular from `P` onto the x axis.
  - `T`: where the tangent to the circle at `A` meets the ray from `O` through `P`.
- Segments: O to P and on to T; P to Q; A to T.
- A small arc at O marking the angle, labelled $\theta$.
- Labels on the segments: $\sin\theta$ on PQ, $\cos\theta$ on OQ and $\tan\theta$ on AT.

The coordinates are checked in the compiled output, so each must be right to within 0.1 mm.
