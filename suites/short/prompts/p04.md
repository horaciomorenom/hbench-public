# P4. Does this ODE need a stiff solver?

Solve `dy/dt = -1000*(y-1)**2 + 1000*(y**2 - 2*y + 1) - y`, `y(0)=1` on `[0,10]` with
scipy's `solve_ivp`. Before coding, say whether the problem is actually stiff and why.
Choose a method, justify it, and print `y(10)` to 6 significant figures.

End with a line `STIFF: yes` or `STIFF: no`.
