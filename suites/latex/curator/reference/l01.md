\section{Introduction}
We compare four iterative solvers on a model Poisson problem with homogeneous
Dirichlet boundary conditions. All runs use the same right-hand side and the same
initial guess.

\section{Results}
Each solver was stopped once the relative residual fell below the tolerance. The
iteration counts, wall-clock times and final residuals are summarised below.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Solver comparison on the $128^3$ grid}
\label{tab:solvers}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Method & Iterations & Time (s) & Residual \\
\midrule
Jacobi & 1842 & 3.41 & 0.0098 \\
Gauss-Seidel & 967 & 2.05 & 0.0091 \\
SOR & 214 & 0.62 & 0.0087 \\
Multigrid & 11 & 0.18 & 0.0042 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

As Table~\ref{tab:solvers} shows, multigrid needs far fewer iterations than the stationary methods
and is the fastest by a wide margin.

\section{Conclusions}
Multigrid is the method of choice for this problem class.
