# P9. Dipole with a source surface (derive, then code)

In spherical coordinates with the Sun at radius r=1, take a potential field `B = -∇Φ`,
axisymmetric, with `B_r(1,θ) = B0 cosθ` and a source surface at `r = R_ss = 2.5` where the
tangential field vanishes (`B_θ(R_ss,θ)=0`). (a) Derive `B_r(r,θ)` and `B_θ(r,θ)` in closed
form. (b) Write Python functions `Br(r, th)` and `Bt(r, th)`. (c) Verify numerically with
finite differences that ∇·B = 0 and ∇×B = 0 at (r, θ) = (1.7, 0.9).

Use `B0 = 1` and `R_ss = 2.5` as module constants; `Br(r, th)` and `Bt(r, th)` must accept numpy arrays or floats.
