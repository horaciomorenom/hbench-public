# P7. Positivity-preserving nonlinear conduction (solver)

Solve `u_t = (κ(u) u_x)_x` on [0,1] with `κ(u)=u^2.5`, insulated (zero-flux) ends,
`u(x,0)=1+2*exp(-((x-0.5)/0.05)**2)`, to t=0.005, in conservative finite-volume form on
N cell-centered cells. Use explicit time stepping with dt from a CFL condition, and
face conductivity as the arithmetic mean of the neighbouring cell values. Write
`conduction(N)` returning `(x, u)`. State the timestep restriction you used and why the
scheme preserves positivity and conserves ∑u.
