\section{Discretisation choices}
Table~\ref{tab:choices} lists the options we considered for the solver.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Options considered for each part of the solver}
\label{tab:choices}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Component & Selected option & Reason for the selection \\
\midrule
Spatial discretisation & Second-order finite volumes on a staggered grid & Conserves mass exactly and avoids spurious pressure modes on uniform meshes \\
Time integration & Third-order strong-stability-preserving Runge-Kutta & Keeps the total variation bounded for the advective terms at moderate cost \\
Linear solver & Conjugate gradients with an algebraic multigrid preconditioner & Iteration counts stay nearly independent of the mesh size in all our tests \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}
\end{table}

The remainder of the report evaluates these choices.
