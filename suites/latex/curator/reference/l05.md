\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
We ran the solver with the option grid\_size set to 128. About 50\% of the runs converged
within the first hundred iterations.
The methods compared were Jacobi \& Gauss-Seidel.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Tolerance of $10^{-6}$ on the relative residual
  \item At most 500 iterations per solve
\end{itemize}

\section{Analysis}
\label{sec:analysis}
With the settings from Section~\ref{sec:setup}, the error satisfies
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:rate}
  e_k \le \frac{1}{1 + k^2} \, e_0.
\end{equation}
By~\eqref{eq:rate} the error decays quadratically. The decay is visible in
Figure~\ref{fig:decay}.

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.5\textwidth]{example-image-a}
  \caption{Error decay over the iterations.}
  \label{fig:decay}
\end{figure}

The total cost was \$1200 per run, which fits our budget.
