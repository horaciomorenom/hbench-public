\section{Model problem}
Let $\Omega$ be a bounded domain. We seek $u$ such that
\begin{align}
  -\Delta u &= f && \text{in } \Omega, \label{eq:pde} \\
  u &= 0 && \text{on } \partial\Omega. \label{eq:bc}
\end{align}
The source term is piecewise
\begin{equation}
  f(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } |x| < \frac{1}{2}, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
  \label{eq:source}
\end{equation}
Multiplying~\eqref{eq:pde} by a test function $v$ and integrating by parts gives
\begin{align}
  \int_\Omega (-\Delta u)\, v \,\mathrm{d}x &= \int_\Omega \nabla u \cdot \nabla v \,\mathrm{d}x \nonumber \\
  &= \int_\Omega f v \,\mathrm{d}x. \label{eq:weak}
\end{align}
On a uniform mesh of size $h$ the element stiffness matrix is
\begin{equation*}
  K = \frac{1}{h} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation*}
Equation~\eqref{eq:weak} holds for every admissible $v$.
