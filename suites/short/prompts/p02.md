# P2. Silo (calculus word problem)

A silo is a cylinder 100 ft high with radius 200 ft. Ore is poured from a conveyor at
60,000π ft³/h and forms a cone whose radius is 1.5 times its height. At a certain time
the pile is 60 ft high. How long, in hours, until the pile reaches the top of the silo?
Give the final answer to 1 decimal place and show the cone-volume derivation.

End with a line `ANSWER: <hours>`.
